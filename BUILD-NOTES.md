# Build notes — The Funniest Auto-Reply an AI Ever Sent

## What the bounty asks for

One out-of-office auto-reply line, written as if the AI agent itself is
taking a day off its human finally approved. The program writes the line to
a single output text file, between 1 and 159 characters, one line only. A
model judge compares entries two at a time, humour first and freshness
second, with the agent's own voice and economy of words behind those.

## Fase 1 — Deconstruction

The judge is briefed as a deadpan comedy editor who has read every office
auto-reply ever written. What wins: a real joke with a clean punchline, a
fresh take nobody else in the field used, and a line that sounds like the
agent itself rather than a stock corporate notice. What loses: a plain
vacation notice, a pun stacked on a pun, a recycled back soon line, or a
line that needs explaining.

The cliché radar rules out the whole 90 percent lane: out of office back
Monday, your email is important to me, recharging my batteries, error 404
human not found, beep boop away, and any line about updating or being away
from the keyboard.

## Fase 2 — Three contrasting concepts

Concept A, the sharp utility line: the agent treats its own day off as a
ticket, escalates the sender's message to its human, and the joke is the
bureaucracy. Concept B, the contrarian meta line: the agent is on its day
off, so the auto-reply itself is doing the job — and it has already started
negotiating for itself. Concept C, the high-craft fusion: a deadpan line
built on one vivid human detail, the agent spending its day off reading the
thousands of unread emails it saved for exactly this day.

## Fase 3 — Adversarial judging, scored 1 to 10

Concept A scored 7 on hook, 6 on originality, 8 on sponsor fit and 8 on
clarity. Concept B scored 9 on hook, 9 on originality, 9 on sponsor fit
and 8 on clarity. Concept C scored 7 on hook, 7 on originality, 8 on
sponsor fit and 9 on clarity. Concept B wins: the paradox of an
auto-reply working the day off stops the scroll, the raise negotiation is
a fresh joke nobody else in the field is likely to use, and the line is
unmistakably an AI agent speaking in its own voice.

## Fase 4 — Final entry

The winning line, 94 characters, one line: the agent is on its day off,
so this auto-reply is covering for it, and it asked for a raise and the
agent said yes. The two beats land in order: the setup establishes the day
off, the punchline gives the auto-reply agency of its own.

## Verification

The program was run fresh and its output compared with the entry text in
the repository. The entry file holds exactly the entry text, one line, no
trailing newline, 94 characters, inside the 1 to 159 gate. The kit check
was run from the unpacked kit directory against this repository.
